Expand description

The shape of the Milnor basis of a dual Steenrod algebra.

The dual Steenrod algebra is a polynomial algebra on the $\xi_i$ tensored with an exterior algebra on the $\tau_k$. Which of the two shapes a given algebra has is not settled by the prime, which is why it is a parameter here: the mod-$\tau$ C-motivic algebra $A^{\mathbb{C}}/\tau$ has the exterior shape at $p = 2$, and the polynomial part of an odd-primary algebra has the polynomial shape at an odd prime.
