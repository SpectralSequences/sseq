fn mul_monomials(m1: Monomial, m2: Monomial, acc: &mut DualElement)
Expand description

Multiply two basis monomials of $A_{**}$ and accumulate coeff * (m1 * m2) into acc.

$A_{**}$ is commutative, so $\tau(E_1)\xi(R_1)\cdot\tau(E_2)\xi(R_2)$ is obtained by adding exponents: the exterior parts form $S = E_1 + E_2$ (entries in ${0,1,2}$), which is rewritten into the square-free basis via rewrite_tau ($\tau_i^2 = \tau\xi_{i+1}$), and the resulting $\xi$ exponents are added to $R_1 + R_2$.
