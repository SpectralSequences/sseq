Expand description

The τ⁰ part of a · b, i.e. their product in the mod-τ reduction A_C/τ.

Equal to filtering multiply_closed down to the terms tau_exponent puts at 0, but it constrains the enumeration instead of the output: mod τ the X matrix must satisfy S(X) = R₁ exactly, which is the classical admissible-matrix condition.
