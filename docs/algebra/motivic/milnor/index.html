The C-motivic dual Steenrod algebra $A_{**}$ and Steenrod algebra $A_C$ in the Milnor basis.

Structs§

Dual
The basis element of $A_C$ dual to a monomial of $A_{**}$.
Monomial
A basis monomial $\tau(E)\xi(R)$ of the dual algebra.
MotivicMilnorAlgebra
The C-motivic (prime 2) Steenrod algebra $A_C$, presented as a free $\mathbb{F}_2[\tau]$-module on the Milnor basis ${Q(E)P(R)}$ with lazy per-(topological-)degree basis indexing.
MotivicTerm
A term $\tau^{\text{tau_pow}} \cdot Q(E) P(R)$ (mod 2, C-motivic so no $\rho$), the output of rewriting a monomial into the Milnor basis. e_mask encodes $E \in \mathrm{Seq}_1$.
ProductBlock
A dense block of basis-element products for one pair of topological degrees (t1, t2). Entry (idx1, idx2), at flat position idx1 * dim2 + idx2, is the product of the idx1-th basis element in degree t1 with the idx2-th in degree t2, as indices into the degree-(t1 + t2) basis.
SparseSum
A set of Ks taken mod 2, as a Vec sorted by key: adding a term toggles it.

Traits§

Bigraded
A monomial carrying a motivic bidegree, in whichever of the two dual conventions its type stands for.

Functions§

antipode
The antipode $\chi$ of $A_{}$ — an algebra map (since $A_{}$ is commutative) extended from chi_tau/chi_xi on the generators. It converts the paper’s conjugate generators to the standard Milnor/Voevodsky generators (χ(ξ_2) = ξ_2 + ξ_1^3, etc.) and is an involution.
coproduct
The coproduct of an arbitrary element of $A_{**}$ (extended $\mathbb{F}_2[\tau]$-linearly).
dual_mul
The product of two elements of $A_{**}$.
dual_one
The unit $1 \in A_{**}$.
enum_basis
All basis monomials τ(E)ξ(R) of a given topological degree target.
multiply
The product a · b in $A_C$, as the set of basis elements occurring — their $\mathbb{F}_2[\tau]$ coefficients being implicit (tau_exponent).
multiply_closed
The product a · b in A_C via Theorem 5.1 of Kong–Lin, Product formulas for motivic Milnor basis (arXiv:2411.12890), at the C-motivic point $\rho = 0$. Same contract as multiply (the duality oracle it is validated against).
multiply_closed_mod_tau
The τ⁰ part of a · b, i.e. their product in the mod-τ reduction A_C/τ.
rewrite_tau
Rewrite $\tau(S) = \tau_0^{s_0}\tau_1^{s_1}\cdots$ (with s an arbitrary exponent vector) into the C-motivic Milnor basis, using $\tau_i^2 = \tau\xi_{i+1}$ (Theorem 3.4 at $\rho = 0$): $$\tau(S) = \sum_{\substack{E \in \mathrm{Seq}_1,\ R \in \mathrm{Seq}\ \Sigma_2(E)+\Sigma_2(R)=\Sigma_2(S)\ \Sigma(E)+2\Sigma(R)=\Sigma(S)}} c(S,R), \tau^{\Sigma(R)}, \tau(E)\xi(R),$$ where the second constraint is the vanishing of the $\rho$-exponent $\Sigma(S) - \Sigma(E) - 2\Sigma(R)$.
tau_exponent
The exponent of $\tau$ on the term z of a product a * b — the coefficient that products here leave unstored.
tau_gen
The generator $\tau_i \in A_{**}$.
xi_gen
The generator $\xi_i \in A_{**}$ (for $i \ge 1$).

Type Aliases§

DualElement
An element of the dual algebra $A_{**}$: a mod-2 set of Monomials, whose $\mathbb{F}_2[\tau]$ coefficients are implicit in the weight (tau_exponent).
SteenrodElement
An element of $A_C$: a mod-2 set of basis elements, whose $\mathbb{F}_2[\tau]$ coefficients are likewise implicit (tau_exponent).
TensorElement
An element of $A_{} \otimes A_{}$: a mod-2 set of pairs of Monomials, coefficients implicit as in DualElement.
